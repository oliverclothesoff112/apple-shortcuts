# Monk Mode

## What is it?

Monk Mode is small automaation system made up of three Apple Shortcuts that work together to make my phone less distracting during the working day.

The goal wasn't to stop me using my phone completely. It was to make intentional use the primary function, and lessen the cahnces of mindless scrolling.

---

## The Problem

During the work day I found myself unlocking my phone out of habit rather than necessity.

Scrolling Instagram or playing a game became a crutch to boredome, sure, but they also became a habitiual barrier to productivity.

Those small interruptions quickly added up and made it harder to stay focused.

---

## The Solution

A single tap activates Monk Mode.

When enabled, the shortcut prepares my phone for focused work by applying my preferred settings and reducing unnecessary distractions.

It can also be triggered using an NFC tag for a frictionless workflow.

---

## Features

-  Reduce distractions
-  Quick to activate
-  Optional NFC tag support
-  Designed for better focus in the workplace
